# Customer Behavior Analysis – Business Insights

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes retail customer shopping behavior to uncover purchasing trends, customer segments, and revenue opportunities. Using SQL, Python, and Power BI, the analysis transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights that support customer engagement, marketing strategies, and revenue optimization.

---

# Dataset Overview

- Retail customer shopping transactions
- Customer demographic information
- Product categories
- Purchase amount
- Customer ratings
- Discount usage
- Shipping preferences
- Subscription status
- Previous purchase history

---

# Business Objectives

The project aims to answer several key business questions:

- Which customer segments generate the highest revenue?
- Which products perform the best?
- Does offering discounts increase customer spending?
- Which customers are likely to become loyal buyers?
- How does age influence purchasing behavior?
- Which shipping methods generate the highest sales?
- What opportunities exist to improve customer retention?

---

# Key Insights

## 1. Customer Segmentation

Customers were segmented into:

- New Customers
- Returning Customers
- Loyal Customers

using previous purchase history.

### Business Value

This segmentation enables businesses to design personalized marketing campaigns and loyalty programs.

---

## 2. Revenue by Gender

Revenue comparison identified which customer segment contributes more to total sales.

### Business Value

Helps marketing teams target campaigns toward higher-value customer groups while identifying opportunities to improve engagement among lower-performing segments.

---

## 3. Discount Impact

The analysis identified customers who:

- Used discounts
- Still spent above the average purchase amount

### Business Value

These customers represent high-value shoppers who respond well to promotional campaigns.

---

## 4. Product Performance

The analysis identified:

- Top-rated products
- Products with the highest discount usage
- Best-selling products within each category

using SQL ranking functions.

### Business Value

Supports inventory planning, pricing strategies, and product promotion decisions.

---

## 5. Customer Loyalty

Customers were classified based on purchase frequency into New, Returning, and Loyal segments.

### Business Value

Allows businesses to develop retention strategies and reward loyal customers.

---

## 6. Revenue by Age Group

Revenue contribution was analyzed across different customer age groups.

### Business Value

Helps businesses understand their most profitable customer demographics and optimize marketing campaigns.

---

## 7. Subscription Analysis

Compared spending behavior between subscribers and non-subscribers.

### Business Value

Provides insights into the effectiveness of subscription programs and customer retention initiatives.

---

## 8. Shipping Preferences

Revenue was analyzed based on shipping methods.

### Business Value

Supports logistics optimization and customer experience improvements.

# Business Recommendations

### 1. Increase Loyalty Rewards

Loyal customers generate consistent revenue. Introduce personalized reward programs to improve retention.

---

### 2. Target High-Value Discount Users

Customers who spend above average despite using discounts are strong candidates for premium offers and personalized promotions.

---

### 3. Optimize Product Promotions

Promote highly rated products while evaluating heavily discounted products for profitability.

---

### 4. Improve Customer Segmentation

Use behavioral segmentation instead of generic campaigns to improve conversion rates.

---

### 5. Focus on High-Revenue Demographics

Allocate marketing budgets toward customer age groups that contribute the highest revenue.

---

### 6. Expand Subscription Programs

If subscribers demonstrate higher spending, invest in subscription-based benefits to improve long-term customer value.
